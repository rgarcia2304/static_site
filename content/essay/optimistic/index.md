## Strokes of genius

Strokes of genius are what we remember from Sports. Michael Jordan’s game winner against the Jazz, The Miracle on Ice, and Usain Bolt’s record breaking 200m dash. These events are captured in the hearts of millions if not billions of individuals world wide. They inspire others to try and replicate them. Thanks to these people, some push hard enough and dream. One thing that has remained true is that sports are harder now than they were previously in history. Yes I know it’s a hot take but it’s true. With every generation the game asks more of people, then in the previous generation. 

What is true about Lionel Messi, Lebron James, and Tom Brady. They are the goats of their respective sports right? Sure, maybe, arguably they are at the pinnacle of peoples lists but what’s most true is that yes they were awesome especially in the clutch but they might be the best examples of the greatest fundamentalists of all time. 

I have played against amazing footballers and it's incredible just how perfect everything they do seems. But they also compete against the best, so the floor is raised with just how good you need to be. Moments of lapsing concentration lose you games just as often as moments of magic. Do the fundamentals well and you will be ok. Magic takes you to the peak, fundamentals provide safe passage in life.

## Freedom 

We all like to be able to do things. Specifically, we like to be able to do things that are cool. Usually it's the case that cool things are hard to do. Imagine you are trying to learn to shoot your arrow out of a moving car on targets . Well let me let you in for a secret, **that is really hard**. You can try over and over and you might even get lucky and do it once. But once is no fun. We like to be able to do cool things frequently. Here you have two paths, either you become a good archer or you get really good at physics with decent archery skills. Either way both those things are going to take years of time to get down at a good level. They will require you to start from the bottom and to build a really good understanding of a bunch of stuff before you can even think of doing this consistently. Actually chances have it you have a really good chance of having a high percentage shot if you only shoot a couple of times because probability is not deterministic in the short term. But in the long term man you will be lucky to have anything above 0.000001%. So let me lay this down nicely for you. You probably can’t do it. But I mean dream if you want to do it once they have a crack at it. If you start approaching life this way though you will not get much done. 

Putting the time in to learn the fundamentals behind hard tasks gives you **freedom**. I won’t go into too much detail here but stuff in life translates really well. If you want to get good at soccer, ** *football ** please learn how to pass a ball first before you even think of going on youtube to learn how to do some insane skill, you will be tremendously more valuable. The thing is also once you can do things you gain something called **confidence**. This cannot be taken away from you and will actually pull you through the difficulties in life. This is freedom, and actually no one can take that away from you. If you have taken the time to work through some fundamental topics, you will be competent in many subjects which is tremendously valuable. Seriously this is why I am so impassioned to work with youth because if we can just get them to take school seriously it opens up so much.

Exercise every day for 1 hr per day is pretty hard and you will be ok. Be kind, don’t lie, and work hard everyday. Put your phone away. All these things are simple but hard.
